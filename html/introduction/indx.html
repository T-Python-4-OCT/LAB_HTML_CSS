Al-Ittihad Saudi Club

About the Club

Al-Ittihad was founded in 1927 in the city of Jeddah, making it the oldest sports club in Saudi Arabia. The club is famously called Al-Amid (The Dean) and The Tigers by its fans.

Club Colors and Stadium

The team wears the traditional Black and Yellow kit and plays home games at King Abdullah Sports City Stadium.

Asian Legacy

Al-Ittihad won back-to-back AFC Champions League titles in 2004 and 2005.

Supporters

The fans are well known across Asia for their attendance and stadium atmosphere.

Motto: Club of the Nation
