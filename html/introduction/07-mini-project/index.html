Irrigation Plan

A simple plan that shows how a water tank feeds three planting zones through one main pipe.

Layout

System components

  • Water tank
  • Main pipe
  • Branch pipes
  • Valves and sprinklers

How the system works

  1. Water flows from the tank into the main pipe.
  2. A valve opens the branch of the chosen zone.
  3. Sprinklers water the plants for the scheduled time.
  4. The valve closes and the next zone starts.
