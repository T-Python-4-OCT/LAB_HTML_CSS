Riyadh weekends

From Qassim to Riyadh.

I moved from the countryside in Qassim to Riyadh. I'm still getting used to the pace of the city and figuring out how I want to spend my weekends.

From rural life to city life

The move means getting used to a different daily rhythm.

  • Life in Qassim: I was used to a quieter pace and more open space.
  • Life in Riyadh: the city feels busier, with more places to explore and a different rhythm to get used to.
  • My weekends now: I want to walk more, visit somewhere new, and find places I enjoy.

I don't need a packed schedule. I just want to feel more at home here.

New habits, one weekend at a time

  1. Go for a walk. Try a weekend walk at المسار الرياضي.
  2. Explore a new place. Make time for عالم البوليفارد on a future weekend.
  3. Keep a short list. Note the places I'd like to revisit.
