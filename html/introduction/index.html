About the Club

Juventus is a professional football club from Turin, Italy, founded in 1897. The team plays in black and white stripes and is nicknamed "The Old Lady". It is one of the most successful clubs in Italian football.

  • City: Turin, Italy
  • Stadium: Allianz Stadium
  • Colors: Black and white

Club Legends

  • Alessandro Del Piero
  • Gianluigi Buffon
  • Michel Platini
  • Zinedine Zidane
