About Real Madrid

Real Madrid Club de Fútbol is a famous football club from Madrid, Spain.

The club is known for its history, achievements, and passionate fans around the world.

Official Website: Visit Real Madrid

Club History

Real Madrid was founded in 1902. It is one of the most successful football clubs in history.
