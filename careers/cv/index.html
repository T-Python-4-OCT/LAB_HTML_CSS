TaskFlow: Task Management System

TaskFlow helps teams organize their work, distribute responsibilities and track progress. It has two types of users, administrators and team members, and gives administrators a clear view of work progress and team performance.

How a task moves through the system

  1. Assign The administrator creates a task and assigns it to a team member.
  2. Work The member updates progress and adds notes.
  3. Submit The member submits the completed task for review.
  4. Review The administrator reviews and approves it, and it is kept in the task history.

System features

  • Manage user accounts, tasks and work groups.
  • Assign tasks to team members and manage task progress.
  • Submit completed tasks for review and approval.
  • View task history and personal reports.
  • Monitor team performance through reports and a dashboard.

Who uses it

TaskFlow user roles and their main actions
Role Main actions
Administrator Manage users and work groups, create and assign tasks, review submissions, monitor progress on the dashboard.
Team member View assigned tasks, update progress, add notes, submit completed work for review.

Result: better team organization, clearer responsibilities, trackable task completion, and a complete overview of work for administrators.
