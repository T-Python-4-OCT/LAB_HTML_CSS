01 / THE BEGINNING

What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a field of computer science focused on developing systems that can perform tasks associated with human intelligence.

These tasks include learning from data, recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making predictions. AI is becoming an important part of our daily lives.

From virtual assistants to advanced medical technologies, artificial intelligence is changing how humans interact with machines.

03 / THE UNKNOWN

The Future of AI

A New Chapter Begins

The future of artificial intelligence offers possibilities in science, creativity, transportation, and communication.

However, AI also raises important challenges. Privacy, fairness, misinformation, and the changing nature of work require responsible development.

Key Opportunities and Challenges

  • Accelerating scientific discoveries.
  • Improving productivity and accessibility.
  • Protecting privacy and sensitive data.
  • Reducing bias in automated decisions.
  • Preparing people for changes in employment.
