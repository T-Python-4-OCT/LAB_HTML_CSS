What is Happening in AI Today?

Artificial Intelligence has grown rapidly over the past few years. Instead of only following simple rules, modern tools can write code, analyze data, summarize documents, and help people finish daily work much faster.

AI tools are helping developers and students build projects faster than ever before.

Main Areas Using AI

Software Development

Helping programmers write, test, and debug code in less time.

Healthcare

Supporting doctors in reading medical scans and organizing patient data.

Education

Giving students personalized explanations and practice examples.

Accessibility

Improving voice recognition, screen readers, and live translation tools.
