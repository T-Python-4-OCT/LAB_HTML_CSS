UNDERSTANDING AI

So, what exactly is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence, or AI, is a field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks normally associated with human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, recognizing images, understanding language, and making predictions.

Instead of following only fixed instructions, many modern AI systems learn patterns from large amounts of data. This allows them to improve their performance and assist people in increasingly useful ways.
