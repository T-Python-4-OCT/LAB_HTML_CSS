01 / THE MOMENT

Introduction

Artificial intelligence has moved from research labs into everyday life. It helps people translate languages, discover patterns in data, and create new ways to solve familiar problems.

AI is not a single machine or a distant prediction. It is a growing collection of tools that learn from examples and assist people with increasingly complex tasks. Understanding its potential means looking at both the technology and the choices we make around it.

02 / THE JOURNEY

A Short History of AI

Decades of research built the foundations for the AI tools people use today.

  1. 1950

    A question of intelligence

    Alan Turing proposes a practical way to consider whether a machine can demonstrate intelligent behavior.

  2. 1956

    A field takes shape

    The Dartmouth workshop gives artificial intelligence its name and brings researchers together around the new field.

  3. 1997

    A landmark match

    IBM's Deep Blue defeats world chess champion Garry Kasparov in a six-game match.

  4. 2022

    AI reaches everyone

    Conversational AI tools bring generative technology to a broad global audience.

06 / WHAT COMES NEXT

The Future of AI

The future of AI will not be shaped by technology alone. It will depend on the people who design it, the communities who use it, and the standards that guide its development.

Building systems that are useful, transparent, and accountable gives us a better chance of sharing their benefits widely. The most important question is not only what AI can do, but what we want it to help us do together.
