What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating machines and systems capable of performing tasks that usually require human intelligence.

These tasks include learning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognizing images and making decisions.

Applications of AI

Artificial intelligence is used in many industries. Some important examples include:

Healthcare

AI helps analyze medical images and supports healthcare professionals.

Education

AI supports personalized learning and educational assistants.

Transportation

AI helps develop autonomous vehicles and improve traffic systems.

Business

AI helps businesses analyze data and automate repetitive tasks.

Benefits of Artificial Intelligence

  • Improves productivity and efficiency.
  • Helps analyze large amounts of data.
  • Automates repetitive tasks.
  • Supports faster decision-making.
  • Provides new solutions to complex problems.
