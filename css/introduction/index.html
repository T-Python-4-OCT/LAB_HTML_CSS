The move

From rural life to city life

The move means getting used to a different daily rhythm.

  • Life in Qassim

    I was used to a quieter pace and more open space.

  • Life in Riyadh

    The city feels busier, with more places to explore and a different rhythm to get used to.

  • My weekends now

    I want to walk more, visit somewhere new, and find places I enjoy.

I do not need a packed schedule. I just want to feel more at home here.
