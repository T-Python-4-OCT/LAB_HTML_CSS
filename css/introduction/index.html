About the Show

Person of Interest is an American crime and science fiction series created by Jonathan Nolan. It started in 2011 and ended in 2016.

The story is about Harold Finch, a genius programmer who built "The Machine", a computer system that watches everything and can predict crimes. Finch hires John Reese, a former CIA agent, to save the people whose numbers the Machine gives them.

  • Genre: Crime, Action, Sci-Fi
  • Seasons: 5
  • Episodes: 103

My Favorite Duo: Shaw & Reese

Shaw and Reese are my favorite duo. They are both strong, calm, and always ready for action. Their talks are short but very funny.

  • They work together without many words.
  • Their action scenes are the best.
  • They joke even in dangerous moments.
  • They always protect each other.

The Best Episode: Season 3, Episode 10

"The Devil's Share"

In the episode before it, the team loses Detective Joss Carter. In "The Devil's Share", John Reese is full of anger and goes to take revenge for her.

  1. The team is sad after losing Carter.
  2. Reese goes alone to find the people responsible.
  3. Finch, Shaw, and Fusco try to stop him.
  4. The episode ends with a very emotional scene.
